Contested Realms is a territory war for Hytale. You form a clan, plant a
stronghold, expand outward claim by claim, and hold what you take against
everyone else on the map.

## What is in the game now

- **Clans and strongholds** — one keep per clan, and every later claim hangs off it.
- **Outposts and plots** — expand your borders, lease land, and let clansfolk work it.
- **Resources and caravans** — material moves by road, and roads can be cut.
- **Combat units and parties** — infantry, archers and cavalry you raise and march.
- **Sieges** — gates with real hit points and keeps that can fall.
- **Successive realms** — finite maps that are won, recorded, and replaced.

## What comes next

The Hub is being dressed properly, realm generation is being shaped region by
region, and balance is tuned live rather than in patch notes. When a realm opens,
the address goes out to the community first.

If you want to be there for the first one, keep an eye on this page.
